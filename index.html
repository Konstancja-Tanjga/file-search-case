File search for a billion-file browser: before and after feedback

I designed a search form, got it rejected for being too complex, and rebuilt it as one bar of conditions above the file list.

Open the prototype after feedback Open the prototype before feedback
  • BriefA simple, ergonomic form to search files by a few basic conditions, in a folder or in all storage. Flow ends at Run.
  • First answerA full product around the form: side panel, three steps, live counts, AI assistant, saved searches, results.
  • FeedbackToo much around the search. A custom file extension was hard to find. The AI added no value.
  • Second answerOne bar: location, conditions as chips, any extension by typing it, Search.

Before what I sent

A side panel with three numbered steps, a count under every condition, an AI field, and the whole file browser around it.

Feedback from the reviewers, and what it changed

Reviewers saidChange
Side elements drew more attention than the searchOne bar above the list. No panel, steps, counts or results.
A custom extension was very hard to searchType “.cram” and press Enter: “Type is .cram”. Any extension works.
The AI assistant felt bolted onRemoved. The brief asked to build with AI, not to add AI.
Location was shown twice and changed in the less visible placeOne chip, first in the bar. It shows the location and changes it.
Research was too long; the prototype was hard to reachThis page, and one link to each prototype.

After the core task in the fewest steps

What I learned

  1. Restraint shows maturity. Every extra weakened the proof that the user reaches the goal fast.
  2. Limiting words are a ceiling. “Simple” and “a few basic conditions” should have cut the scope on day one.
  3. Solve the brief’s own example first, then its nearest variant: any extension, not only PDF.
  4. One fact, one place, editable where it is shown.
  5. With AI tools, my job is editing. AI made adding cheap; I set a budget and cut.
  6. Five minutes with one fresh person would have caught the extension and location problems.
